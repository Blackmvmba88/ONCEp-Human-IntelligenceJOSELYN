# Product Naming Canon

## PONCE

**PONCE** is the permanent name of the Human Intelligence Platform.

PONCE represents the complete product surface and runtime:

- Human Resources operations
- Talent acquisition
- People Core
- Organizational intelligence
- Event-driven automation
- Temporal orchestration
- Rules and workflows
- Document intelligence
- Security and audit
- Analytics
- AI assistance
- Digital twin modeling

## JOSELYN CLI

**JOSELYN CLI** is the permanent name of the command-line interface for PONCE.

It is intended for:

- administration
- diagnostics
- automation
- scripting
- developer operations
- audits
- batch execution
- system inspection

## Naming rule

The names PONCE and JOSELYN CLI are product identities, not temporary references to any current collaborator or external person. They remain valid independently of future participation by third parties.
